Heye Huang is an Assistant Professor at the Cho Chun Shik Graduate School of Mobility, KAIST, where he leads the Autonomous Cognitive and Embodied Systems Lab (ACES Lab).

His research focuses on trustworthy safety-critical systems, generative AI and world models, and foundation models and agents for autonomous driving and robotics.

Before joining KAIST, he was a Postdoctoral Associate at the MIT SMART Center and a Research Associate at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. He received his PhD in Mechanical Engineering from Tsinghua University and was a visiting scholar at TU Delft.
